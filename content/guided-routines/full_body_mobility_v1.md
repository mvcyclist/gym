Settle in and breathe. Soften the jaw. [pause:3s]

Settle in. Slow exhales. [pause:3s]

Shoulders melt down. [pause:3s]

## TRX Lat Stretch

Arms straight overhead, straps taut. Sit the hips back and drop the chest toward the floor — long spine, weight in the heels.

Settle in. Breathe into the sides of the ribs. [pause:3s]

[pause:3s]

Keep breathing — soften the ribs down.

Arms stay long — don't shrug. [pause:3s]

## TRX Hamstring Stretch

One heel slightly forward. Hold the handles and hinge at the hips with a flat back.

First side. Settle in — micro-bend the knee if you need it. [pause:3s]

[every5:60]

[pause:3s]

Switch sides — other leg forward, same flat-back hinge.

Find the stretch, not the strain. [pause:3s]

[every5:60]

[pause:3s]

Last hold — hip flexor lunge.

## TRX Hip Flexor Lunge + Overhead Reach
