## Introduction

Warm-up — four dynamic movements, about five minutes. Activation only, not static stretching. Move with control and follow the counts.

## Arm circles

Arm circles. Stand tall, arms out to your sides. Small controlled circles forward — core gently braced, shoulders relaxed.

Here we go. Stay loose through the neck. [pause:3s]

[every5:35]

Halfway — reverse direction. Same size circles, the other way.

Keep the rhythm smooth. [pause:3s]

[every5:35]

Nice. Next — leg swings. Grab something stable if you need balance.

## Leg swings

Hold a rack or wall if you want it. Swing one leg forward and back from the hip — controlled, not ballistic.

Begin. Tall posture — let the leg swing, don't throw it. [pause:3s]

[every5:30]

Switch sides — same control.

Find your balance again. Easy tempo. [pause:3s]

[every5:30]

Good. Next — bodyweight squats.

## Bodyweight squat to stand

Feet about hip-width. Sit down slowly, then drive through your whole foot to stand. Smooth — no bounce at the bottom.

Begin. Chest stays proud. [pause:3s]

[every5:70]

Steady reps, full range. [pause:3s]

Strong. Last one — glute bridges.

## Glute bridges

On your back, feet flat, knees bent. Drive through your heels, squeeze at the top, lower with control.

Begin. Pause one beat at the top each rep. [pause:3s]

[every5:70]

Squeeze hard at the top — control the way down. [pause:3s]

## Outro

Warm-up complete. You're ready for main lifts.
